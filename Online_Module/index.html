WELCOME TO CYBER SAFE

Your First Line of Defence Is Awareness

Every day, individuals and organisations receive messages designed to deceive them. A single careless click can expose passwords, financial information and confidential business data.

This training will help you recognise the warning signs, think critically and make safer decisions online.
